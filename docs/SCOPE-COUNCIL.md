# Buddy — scope & positioning note (council + design)

**Date:** 2026-09-23  
**Inputs:** Lock **Proposal A** (companion) · prefer cuter Buddy **holding a carrot** · tagline candidate **“what’s up, bud?”** · feature ambition: meal planning, gym, workouts, overall health.

## Verdict (chair)

| Move | Fine? | Note |
|------|-------|------|
| A + carrot prop | **Yes** | Distinctive asset; keep night/premium, not kids-app candy |
| “what’s up, bud?” | **As voice / greeting** | Not as sole App Store subtitle — pair with a job line |
| Calorie / plate watch as wedge | **Must keep** | Category buyers understand this |
| Meal plan + gym + workouts + “health overall” at once | **Overboard for v1** | Fine as **year-2 rooms** under one Buddy, not launch IA |

## Recommended wedge

**Buddy = your food & energy buddy** (photo-first plate watch → calories).  
Gym / workouts / meal plans = **later rooms** once daily plate watching sticks.

## Tagline stack

- **Greeting / character:** What’s up, bud?  
- **Job / store:** Watch what you eat. *(or)* Food & energy, with Buddy.

## Carrot rules

- Prop of the character, not a “eat rabbit food” brand  
- Never shame; carrot ≠ moral food  
- Same bone/needle night system as Proposal A  

## Ship order (product design)

1. **MVP** — Today energy · photo plate · XP/streak · Buddy line  
2. **v1.1** — Fast re-log · simple meal plan “tonight”  
3. **v1.2** — Workout log (sets or Apple Health pull)  
4. **Later** — Full meal planning + gym programs  

Do not ship a 4-tab Health OS on day one.
