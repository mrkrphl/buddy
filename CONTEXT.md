## Positioning

Watch what you eat — calories as scoreboard, Buddy as companion. Not MyFitnessPal clone; not guilt coach. **Buddy is a soft marshmallow ghost** who floats beside your plate (flat 2D + carrot).

## Competitive alternatives (status quo)

MFP / Cronometer / Lose It / Notes / doing nothing.

## MVP scope

Today ring · plate photo → Vision describe → FM estimate → confirm · XP/streak · settings target · offline fallback.

## Later rooms (not v1)

Meal planning · gym / workouts · broader health.
