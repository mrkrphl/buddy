How Buddy works

Plan a meal, then log it when it’s real — usually from a plate photo. Buddy estimates calories on your iPhone and keeps today’s energy, your kitchen, and your chat on the device. There is no account.

Get help

Email mrkrphl@gmail.com for logging, camera or photo library access, on-device estimates, or the daily calorie target.

Estimates are approximate and depend on the photo and what you confirm. Buddy is not a doctor or dietitian. For a medical question, see a clinician.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove local Buddy data from that device. There is no cloud copy to erase.
