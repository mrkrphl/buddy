Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Overview

Buddy (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a photo-first calorie companion for iPhone, made by Night Folio. This policy describes what the app handles. It matches the App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

No account · no cloud

Buddy does not require an account. Night Folio does not operate a server for your photos, meals, chat, or settings. We do not sell data, show ads, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Information on your iPhone

The app stores this on your device:

  • Plate logs — title, calories, protein, carbs, fat, a short note, and the photo you logged, saved as a JPEG on the device.
  • Plans — meal titles and planned calories you enter.
  • Chat — messages you send to Buddy and the replies, stored on the device.
  • Kitchen and settings — appliances you stock, optional notes, and your daily calorie target.
  • Progress — a local streak and experience count tied to logging.

Camera and photo library access are used only when you choose to log a plate. You can log from a photo you already have if you do not want to use the camera.

On-device estimates

Calorie estimates and Buddy’s replies run on your iPhone with Apple’s on-device Foundation Models when Apple Intelligence is available. If it is not, Buddy uses a local fallback on the device. Night Folio does not receive the photo or the conversation.

What we do not collect

Buddy does not include analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not ask to track you. Nothing in the app is sent to Night Folio.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove Buddy’s local data from that iPhone. There is no cloud copy to erase.
